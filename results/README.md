# Derived results

These small CSV files reproduce the paper-facing summary values. They are derived outputs, not third-party raw data.

- `data_summary.csv`: affected-sample counts used in the paper.
- `local_effects.csv`: corrected series-weighted product-cluster bootstrap effects.
- `rank_stability.csv`: corrected Kendall tau and winner-change summaries.
- `coverage_effects.csv`: primary eligibility-loss summaries.

Full intermediate outputs are generated by the notebooks and are intentionally not committed.
